A Message from Roberto Treviño

Every generation, the transistors on an integrated circuit shrink once more, but the gap between what a researcher designs and what a fab can actually print widens instead.

I founded this company to help a designer at the workstation see through the eyes of the engineer operating the tools at the bays — with computational lithography as the common ground between them.

I want brilliant work to become physical reality.

— Roberto Treviño
